The Judgment of Arceus

Minute 30: Arceus takes you to a round arena (768 px radius) you cannot leave. It is invincible and a legendary shows up every 5 s. The ranking counts how long you last and how much damage you deal to it. Everything here comes from the game’s code.

  • Pure % HP damage: ignores types, defense, Armor, ability and invulnerability. Only dodging, regeneration and the Focus Sash offset it.
  • You cannot outrun it: it starts at 74% of your speed and after 37 s runs at ×1.22. After 5 min it accelerates without limit.
  • Soft cap at 130: anything above counts 25%.
  • Its pauses (1–1.5 s after each attack) are your distance.
  • Minute 35: it breaks your item.

The legendaries

  • One every 5 s out of 86, each with +25% HP and +15% damage over the previous one.
  • The first one’s HP is 3 s of your damage: your DPS barely matters; from the third on they pile up.
  • They run at least at 80% of your speed. Psychic, Electric, Ghost, Fairy, Fire, Water, Ice and Dragon shoot from 300 px; the rest charge.
  • Their damage does go through your types, defense, Armor (80% cap) and ability.
  • Slows land at 100%, stuns at 35% (a stunned one deals no contact damage). Knockback does nothing.
  • Quick Attack leaves you 0.3 s invulnerable to their hits (not to Arceus’).

Damage to Arceus

5% of it counts (1% after 10 min) and you only target it with no legendary alive. It does not extend the Judgment.

What to do

  1. Arrive high-level: farm before minute 30. Every extra level gives uncapped Vigor or Synthesis, and your level is what explains survival most.
  2. Keep moving: leave every circle as soon as it appears and gain distance during its pauses.
  3. Distance: 250–400 px from Arceus (~200 px gap between beams) and off the edge.
  4. Legendaries on you: Quick Attack through them; slow or stun the group, do not try to kill them after the third.
  5. Cards: Reflexes, Armor, Vigor and Synthesis; Sprint only up to 130.
  6. Item: Quick Claw, Leftovers or Focus Sash (until minute 35).
