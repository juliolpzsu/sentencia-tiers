How Arceus’ Judgment works

Everything here comes from the game’s code (systems/judgment.js and friends), not from anyone’s impressions.

What happens at minute 30

The normal run ends. Arceus takes you to the Ruins of Judgment, a round arena 768 px in radius (24 tiles) nobody can leave. Arceus is invincible, carries a random Plate that sets its type, and every 5 seconds a legendary shows up. The ranking has two halves: how long you last and how much damage you deal to Arceus.

Arceus

  • You cannot outrun it. Its speed is relative to yours: it starts at 74% and after 37 s it runs at 1.22× your speed. After 5 minutes of Judgment it accelerates without limit.
  • Soft speed cap: anything above 130 counts only 25%. Fully stacked you end up around 170 instead of 290.
  • Its pauses are your distance. Every cast freezes it for 1.05–1.45 s, and between casts it only walks 1.4 s. If you always move away you gain about 100 px per cycle until minute 35.
  • Its damage is a percentage of HP and pure: it ignores types, defense, Armor and abilities. Pillars 30%, cross beams 35%, shockwave 40%, touch 40% (rising to 100% between minutes 37 and 40). Only regeneration, dodging and the Focus Sash offset it.
  • Pillars (the Judgment): 4 to 10, radius 44 px, 1.25 s warning; the first one lands exactly where you stand. After the first hit you get 0.45 s of invulnerability that absorbs the rest of the volley.
  • Cross beams: 4, and 8 after 30 s, 34 px wide with a 1.1 s warning, sweeping the whole arena. At 300 px from Arceus the gap between beams is about 200 px; right next to it there is no gap.
  • Shockwave: only if you are closer than 210 px, radius 170, 0.85 s warning. Never get that close.
  • Minute 35: Arceus breaks everyone’s item (Focus Sash included).

The legendaries

  • One every 5 s, random among the game’s 86. Each has 25% more HP and 15% more damage than the previous one.
  • The first one’s HP is 3 s of your own single-target damage (measured before minute 30). That is why your DPS barely matters: from the third one on they pile up no matter what.
  • They run at least at 80% of your speed. Psychic, Electric, Ghost, Fairy, Fire, Water, Ice or Dragon types shoot from 300 px; the rest charge.
  • Their damage does go through your types, your defense (physical or special, by their strong side), Armor (80% cap) and your ability. Contact: 126 × 1.15ⁿ at minute 30.
  • They give experience (nothing else): each one levels you up, and the Zidra Berry heals 45%.
The only things that work on them: slows land at 100% (Ice Beam drops them to 30% speed and with reduced cooldowns it is permanent; with Serene Grace, 20%) and stuns last 35%, but with Reflexes they chain, and a stunned legendary neither attacks nor hurts you on contact. Knockback does nothing, whirlpools only pull 25%, and poison and burn deal a flat 1.5.

Damage to Arceus

Only 5% of what you deal counts, decaying to 1% after 10 minutes, and your attacks only target it when no legendary is alive. It is the second ranking; it does nothing for lasting longer.

What to do

  1. Stay 250–400 px from Arceus and run wide circles along the edge. Gain distance during its pauses.
  2. When the legendaries bunch up, stun and slow the group; do not try to kill them after the third.
  3. Hold Leftovers (or a Focus Sash), stack Reflexes, Armor and Synthesis, and Sprint only up to the 130 cap.
  4. Before the run: Ban Tackle, Quick Attack and Body Slam, and use Starting Move + Repertoire to open with your slow and your stun.
