Co-op

The front-page tier list is for playing solo. In a group more things count: this page weighs them and suggests teams.

What changes in a group

  • Experience is shared. There is a single team level: whatever anyone kills levels everyone. With two players each enemy gives 62.5% of the solo amount (split by 1 + 0.6 per extra player), so a partner who kills as much as you makes the team level 25% faster than you alone. And the level you reach minute 30 with is what explains Judgment survival most.
  • Control protects everyone. A slowed or stunned enemy is so for the whole team, and a stunned one does no contact damage.
  • Revives. A downed partner gets up if you stand next to them for 3 s: dying is not the end while someone stands, and speed helps to get there.
  • More enemies. They have more HP and spawn more the more of you there are.
  • In the Judgment Arceus chases the nearest player and the pillar rain falls on each one: splitting up spreads the pressure.

How it is scored

Score = 45% own survival (the tier list’s estimated seconds) + 25% area farming (horde damage of the slot-1 group) + 20% control (slows and stuns) + 10% speed (reaching revives, acting as bait). Each part as a percentile across the 966 (100 = best). A Pokémon that is average solo but clears or controls well rises here; one that only survives but adds little to the team drops a bit. The support weight (45% between farming and control) is an estimate reasoned from the game rules, not a measurement: group runs cannot be simulated.
