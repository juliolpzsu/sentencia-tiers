Co-op

In a group, experience is shared (with a partner who kills as much as you, the team levels up 25% faster), control protects everyone, a downed partner gets back up after you stand next to them for 3 s, and Arceus chases the nearest player, so splitting up spreads the pressure. That is why farming, control and speed count here too: before minute 30 there is no speed cap, and a fast Pokémon plays support, first to revive and to the chests.
