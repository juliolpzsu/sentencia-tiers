Verdict: Lasts a while in the Judgment, but others do it better with less effort. Mostly a prediction: few real runs. Among the worst at clearing hordes: reaching minute 30 is the real challenge.

Strategy

Judgment tier B and horde tier D: it reaching minute 30 is already a challenge. Weak to Steel (×1.55), Ghost (×1.45) and Poison (×1.38). Resists Dragon (×0.28), Fighting (×0.38) and Psychic (×0.57).

Before minute 30, farm: the level you arrive with is what extends the Judgment most. Fuse Confusion, Nasty Plot, Mist Ball and Draining Kiss in slot 1 at every rift; keep Quick Attack in its own slot for when you get surrounded. Once the group is complete, skip further rifts.

Already at the 130 speed cap.

Kecleon Shop: Thunder Wave (TM), Modest Mint and Technician ability (+2 s in the Judgment).
