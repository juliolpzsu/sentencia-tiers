Verdict: Marginal in the Judgment. A prediction: no real Judgment runs of it. Weak at clearing hordes: reaching minute 30 is hard.

Strategy

Judgment tier C and horde tier C: it struggles to reach minute 30. Weak to Fighting (×1.73), Rock (×1.08) and Ground (×1.08). Resists Ghost (×0.32).

Before minute 30, farm: the level you arrive with is what extends the Judgment most. Fuse Hyper Voice, Swords Dance and Hyper Beam in slot 1 at every rift; keep Quick Attack in its own slot for when you get surrounded. Once the group is complete, skip further rifts.

Already at the 130 speed cap.

Kecleon Shop: Shadow Ball (TM) and Thunder Wave (TM) (+2 s in the Judgment).
