Verdict: Does not last in the Judgment: it lacks the tools and the resistance. Play it for fun, not for the ranking. Clears hordes very well: reaches minute 30 without trouble.

Strategy

Quilava is Judgment tier D (rank 758 of 966) and horde tier A: it reaches minute 30 without trouble with a normal build. Against the legendary pool it is frail against the legendaries (it takes ×1.05 of the reference damage). What punishes it most: Rock (×1.75), Ground (×1.75), Water (×1.70). What it resists best: Ice (×0.64), Fire (×0.64), Fairy (×0.65).

It has neither a natural ranged slow nor a stun: against the legendaries it can only run and endure, so the Judgment comes down to dodging and regeneration. The Kecleon Shop changes that (see the tab).

Speed: with Stride maxed it already touches the soft cap of 130; Arceus always runs at 1.22× your speed, so speed is for dodging and for keeping the legendaries (at 80% of your speed) off you, not for outrunning Arceus. It is medium-sized.

Plan: stay 250–400 px from Arceus (its shockwave only fires if you are closer than 210), run wide circles along the arena edge and gain distance during its casting pauses (1.1–1.5 s every cast). When the legendaries bunch up, keep running: do not try to kill them after the third. From minute 35 Arceus breaks your item and speeds up; from minute 37 its touch hurts more and more. A realistic record sits between 5 and 8 minutes of Judgment.

With the Kecleon Shop it lands at rank 851 (it improves, but others improve more): Timid nature, Flash Fire ability.

Assault Vest−20% damage taken. Helps reach minute 30; in the Judgment it becomes redundant once Armor hits the 80% cap.

Only one per run: dropped by bosses and locked chests.

Level-up cards: priority

  1. Reflexes (−8% cooldown, up to 10): with low cooldowns the slows and stuns chain without gaps.
  2. Sprint: with Stride maxed it already sits at the soft cap of 130; one or two more still help you dodge.
  3. Armor (up to 12): the reduction stacks up to the 80% cap and saves the most HP against the legendaries.
  4. Vigor (+20 HP): margin against the legendaries’ hits, which scale 15% every 5 s.
  5. Synthesis (+0.9 HP/s): recover between pillar volleys (Arceus’ damage is percentage-based, only regeneration offsets it).
  6. Zidra Berry when below half: heals 45% instantly.
  7. Power at least once: it is the condition for Ember and Hyper Beam to evolve at max level.
  8. Reflexes at least once: it is the condition for Flamethrower to evolve at max level.
  9. Power (up to 40) afterwards: damage barely matters in the Judgment. Magnet and Learning only if nothing else is left.

Move evolutions

  • Ember evolves at max level if you have taken the Power card at least once this run.
  • Flamethrower evolves at max level if you have taken the Reflexes card at least once this run.
  • Hyper Beam evolves at max level if you have taken the Power card at least once this run.
