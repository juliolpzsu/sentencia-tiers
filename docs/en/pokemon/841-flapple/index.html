Verdict: Does not last in the Judgment: it lacks the tools and the resistance. Play it for fun, not for the ranking. Among the worst at clearing hordes: reaching minute 30 is the real challenge.

Strategy

Flapple is Judgment tier D (rank 665 of 966) and horde tier D: it reaching minute 30 is already a challenge; the Judgment is almost a bonus. Against the legendary pool it is frail against the legendaries (it takes ×1.04 of the reference damage). What punishes it most: Ice (×2.51), Flying (×1.73), Poison (×1.68). What it resists best: Grass (×0.35), Electric (×0.37), Water (×0.38).

Its natural Judgment tools: Dragon Breath slows the legendaries by 25% (they run at 80% of your speed: that is enough to keep them off you). Bosses ignore knockback and resist whirlpools, but slows land at 100% and stuns chain with Reflexes: with that you can keep the legendaries at bay while you dodge Arceus.

Speed: with Stride maxed it already touches the soft cap of 130; Arceus always runs at 1.22× your speed, so speed is for dodging and for keeping the legendaries (at 80% of your speed) off you, not for outrunning Arceus. It is big: it counts as the maximum radius (18 px) and takes slightly longer to leave a pillar.

Plan: stay 250–400 px from Arceus (its shockwave only fires if you are closer than 210), run wide circles along the arena edge and gain distance during its casting pauses (1.1–1.5 s every cast). When the legendaries bunch up, keep Dragon Breath active on the group. From minute 35 Arceus breaks your item and speeds up; from minute 37 its touch hurts more and more. A realistic record sits between 5 and 8 minutes of Judgment.

With the Kecleon Shop it lands at rank 796 (it improves, but others improve more): Jolly nature, Gluttony ability.

Only one per run: dropped by bosses and locked chests.

Level-up cards: priority

  1. Reflexes (−8% cooldown, up to 10): with low cooldowns the slows and stuns chain without gaps.
  2. Sprint: with Stride maxed it already sits at the soft cap of 130; one or two more still help you dodge.
  3. Armor (up to 12): the reduction stacks up to the 80% cap and saves the most HP against the legendaries.
  4. Vigor (+20 HP): margin against the legendaries’ hits, which scale 15% every 5 s.
  5. Synthesis (+0.9 HP/s): recover between pillar volleys (Arceus’ damage is percentage-based, only regeneration offsets it).
  6. Zidra Berry when below half: heals 45% instantly.
  7. Power at least once: it is the condition for Dragon Breath to evolve at max level.
  8. Synthesis at least once: it is the condition for Energy Ball to evolve at max level.
  9. Reflexes at least once: it is the condition for Razor Leaf to evolve at max level.
  10. Power (up to 40) afterwards: damage barely matters in the Judgment. Magnet and Learning only if nothing else is left.

Move evolutions

  • Dragon Breath evolves at max level if you have taken the Power card at least once this run.
  • Energy Ball evolves at max level if you have taken the Synthesis card at least once this run.
  • Razor Leaf evolves at max level if you have taken the Reflexes card at least once this run.
