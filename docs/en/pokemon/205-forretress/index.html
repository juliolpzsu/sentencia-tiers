Verdict: A very good Judgment pick, though it lacks a control tool.

Strategy

Forretress is Judgment tier A (rank 31 of 966) and horde tier B: it reaches minute 30 if the run goes cleanly. Against the legendary pool it shrugs off the legendaries’ hits (it takes ×0.75 of the reference damage). What punishes it most: Fire (×2.33), Flying (×1.04), Water (×1.00). What it resists best: Grass (×0.29), Poison (×0.30), Bug (×0.48).

Its natural Judgment tools: String Shot slows the legendaries by 60%. Bosses ignore knockback and resist whirlpools, but slows land at 100% and stuns chain with Reflexes: with those the legendaries stop being a threat and all that is left is dodging Arceus.

Speed: it needs about 2 Sprint cards to reach the soft cap of 130; Arceus always runs at 1.22× your speed, so speed is for dodging and for keeping the legendaries (at 80% of your speed) off you, not for outrunning Arceus. It is medium-sized.

Plan: stay 250–400 px from Arceus (its shockwave only fires if you are closer than 210), run wide circles along the arena edge and gain distance during its casting pauses (1.1–1.5 s every cast). When the legendaries bunch up, keep String Shot active on the group. From minute 35 Arceus breaks your item and speeds up; from minute 37 its touch hurts more and more. A realistic record sits between 5 and 8 minutes of Judgment.

With the Kecleon Shop it climbs to rank 13: Thunder Wave via TM, Jolly nature.

Level-up cards: priority

  1. Reflexes (−8% cooldown, up to 10): with low cooldowns the slows and stuns chain without gaps.
  2. Sprint: about 2 cards to reach the soft cap of 130 with Stride maxed.
  3. Armor (up to 12): the reduction stacks up to the 80% cap and saves the most HP against the legendaries.
  4. Vigor (+20 HP): margin against the legendaries’ hits, which scale 15% every 5 s.
  5. Synthesis (+0.9 HP/s): recover between pillar volleys (Arceus’ damage is percentage-based, only regeneration offsets it).
  6. Zidra Berry when below half: heals 45% instantly.
  7. Reflexes at least once: it is the condition for String Shot to evolve at max level.
  8. Power at least once: it is the condition for Meteor Mash to evolve at max level.
  9. Armor at least once: it is the condition for Flash Cannon to evolve at max level.
  10. Power (up to 40) afterwards: damage barely matters in the Judgment. Magnet and Learning only if nothing else is left.

Move evolutions

  • String Shot evolves at max level if you have taken the Reflexes card at least once this run.
  • Meteor Mash evolves at max level if you have taken the Power card at least once this run.
  • Flash Cannon evolves at max level if you have taken the Armor card at least once this run.
