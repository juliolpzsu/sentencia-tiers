Verdict: Marginal for the Judgment: it lacks control or toughness and needs a clean run.

Strategy

Hatterene is Judgment tier C (rank 441 of 966) and horde tier D: it reaching minute 30 is already a challenge; the Judgment is almost a bonus. Against the legendary pool it takes the legendaries’ hits about as well as average (it takes ×0.82 of the reference damage). What punishes it most: Steel (×1.46), Ghost (×1.45), Poison (×1.45). What it resists best: Dragon (×0.27), Fighting (×0.33), Psychic (×0.55).

Its natural Judgment tools: Mist Ball slows the legendaries by 30%; Aqua Ring gives it regeneration against the pillars. Bosses ignore knockback and resist whirlpools, but slows land at 100% and stuns chain with Reflexes: with those the legendaries stop being a threat and all that is left is dodging Arceus.

Speed: it needs about 3 Sprint cards to reach the soft cap of 130; Arceus always runs at 1.22× your speed, so speed is for dodging and for keeping the legendaries (at 80% of your speed) off you, not for outrunning Arceus. It is big: it counts as the maximum radius (18 px) and takes slightly longer to leave a pillar.

Plan: stay 250–400 px from Arceus (its shockwave only fires if you are closer than 210), run wide circles along the arena edge and gain distance during its casting pauses (1.1–1.5 s every cast). When the legendaries bunch up, keep Mist Ball active on the group. From minute 35 Arceus breaks your item and speeds up; from minute 37 its touch hurts more and more. A realistic record sits between 5 and 8 minutes of Judgment.

With the Kecleon Shop it climbs to rank 321: Thunder Wave via TM, Bold nature, Magic Bounce ability.

Level-up cards: priority

  1. Reflexes (−8% cooldown, up to 10): with low cooldowns the slows and stuns chain without gaps.
  2. Sprint: about 3 cards to reach the soft cap of 130 with Stride maxed.
  3. Armor (up to 12): the reduction stacks up to the 80% cap and saves the most HP against the legendaries.
  4. Vigor (+20 HP): margin against the legendaries’ hits, which scale 15% every 5 s.
  5. Synthesis (+0.9 HP/s): recover between pillar volleys (Arceus’ damage is percentage-based, only regeneration offsets it).
  6. Zidra Berry when below half: heals 45% instantly.
  7. Reflexes at least once: it is the condition for Mist Ball and Confusion to evolve at max level.
  8. Synthesis at least once: it is the condition for Draining Kiss to evolve at max level.
  9. Power (up to 40) afterwards: damage barely matters in the Judgment. Magnet and Learning only if nothing else is left.

Move evolutions

  • Mist Ball evolves at max level if you have taken the Reflexes card at least once this run.
  • Draining Kiss evolves at max level if you have taken the Synthesis card at least once this run.
  • Confusion evolves at max level if you have taken the Reflexes card at least once this run.
