Verdict: Lasts a while in the Judgment, but others do it better with less effort. It is also among the best at clearing hordes: reaches minute 30 with ease.

Strategy

Great Tusk is Judgment tier B (rank 144 of 966) and horde tier S: it reaches minute 30 comfortably. Against the legendary pool it is frail against the legendaries (it takes ×1.08 of the reference damage). What punishes it most: Flying (×1.81), Water (×1.67), Ice (×1.64). What it resists best: Electric (×0.29), Rock (×0.30), Bug (×0.49).

Its natural Judgment tools: Mud Shot slows the legendaries by 40% (they run at 80% of your speed: that is enough to keep them off you); Dynamic Punch stuns them in zones. Bosses ignore knockback and resist whirlpools, but slows land at 100% and stuns chain with Reflexes: with those the legendaries stop being a threat and all that is left is dodging Arceus. If you earn a bond in a rift, fuse the slow and the stun into one slot so both stay active at once.

Speed: with Stride maxed it already touches the soft cap of 130; Arceus always runs at 1.22× your speed, so speed is for dodging and for keeping the legendaries (at 80% of your speed) off you, not for outrunning Arceus. It is big: it counts as the maximum radius (18 px) and takes slightly longer to leave a pillar.

Plan: stay 250–400 px from Arceus (its shockwave only fires if you are closer than 210), run wide circles along the arena edge and gain distance during its casting pauses (1.1–1.5 s every cast). When the legendaries bunch up, drop Dynamic Punch on them and keep Mud Shot active on the group. From minute 35 Arceus breaks your item and speeds up; from minute 37 its touch hurts more and more. A realistic record sits between 5 and 8 minutes of Judgment.

With the Kecleon Shop it lands at rank 418 (it improves, but others improve more): Jolly nature.

Abilities

Protosynthesis Ability 1 · defaultIn sun, +20% damage. +8% damage with Ground-type moves.

Each account rolls ability 1 or 2 at random (50%), and the hidden one 1 in 20. The Ability Capsule (1,500) swaps between 1 and 2; the Hidden Ability Capsule (5,000) sets the hidden one.

WhySlows everything it touches by 40% and bosses do not resist it: the Judgment legendaries drop to 60% speed and stop catching you. With Reflexes the cooldown gets so short the slow never lapses.

A tremendous punch where the ground warns: it stuns whoever it catches.
WhyTelegraphed zone that stuns. On bosses it only lasts 35% (0.66 s per cast), but with the reduced cooldown it chains: a stunned legendary neither attacks nor hurts you on contact.

Bonemerang Ground boomerangPhysical
A bone that flies out and back, hitting hard.
WhyYour damage move to reach minute 30 and finish legendaries: it is physical, its strong side (1.0× of max), and hits on the way out and back. Damage matters little in the Judgment (each legendary’s HP scales with your own damage), so reliability comes first.

Swords Dance Normal buff
Deals no damage: builds (physical) ATTACK while active.
WhyStacks Attack (up to 5) for same-side moves. Irrelevant against Arceus, but it shortens the waves before minute 30 and the first legendaries.

Held item

Leftovers RecommendedRecover 1.5% of your HP per second. Arceus’ damage is percentage-based and pure: percentage regeneration is the only thing that offsets it.
Focus SashA lethal hit leaves you at 1 HP (every 60 s). Saves you from a pillar or an Arceus touch until minute 35, when Arceus breaks your item.
Assault Vest−20% damage taken. Helps reach minute 30; in the Judgment it becomes redundant once Armor hits the 80% cap.

Only one per run: dropped by bosses and locked chests.

Level-up cards: priority

  1. Reflexes (−8% cooldown, up to 10): with low cooldowns the slows and stuns chain without gaps.
  2. Sprint: with Stride maxed it already sits at the soft cap of 130; one or two more still help you dodge.
  3. Armor (up to 12): the reduction stacks up to the 80% cap and saves the most HP against the legendaries.
  4. Vigor (+20 HP): margin against the legendaries’ hits, which scale 15% every 5 s.
  5. Synthesis (+0.9 HP/s): recover between pillar volleys (Arceus’ damage is percentage-based, only regeneration offsets it).
  6. Zidra Berry when below half: heals 45% instantly.
  7. Power at least once: it is the condition for Mud Shot and Dynamic Punch and Bonemerang to evolve at max level.
  8. Power (up to 40) afterwards: damage barely matters in the Judgment. Magnet and Learning only if nothing else is left.
