Verdict: Not a Judgment pick: it lacks the tools and the toughness. Play it for fun, not for the ranking.

Strategy

Skwovet is Judgment tier D (rank 961 of 966) and horde tier D: it reaching minute 30 is already a challenge; the Judgment is almost a bonus. Against the legendary pool it is frail against the legendaries (it takes ×1.19 of the reference damage). What punishes it most: Fighting (×1.78), Flying (×1.25), Water (×1.21). What it resists best: Ghost (×0.35).

It has neither a natural slow nor a stun: against the legendaries it can only run and endure, so the Judgment comes down to dodging and regeneration. The Kecleon Shop changes that (see the tab).

Speed: it needs about 3 Sprint cards to reach the soft cap of 130; Arceus always runs at 1.22× your speed, so speed is for dodging and for keeping the legendaries (at 80% of your speed) off you, not for outrunning Arceus. It is medium-sized.

Plan: stay 250–400 px from Arceus (its shockwave only fires if you are closer than 210), run wide circles along the arena edge and gain distance during its casting pauses (1.1–1.5 s every cast). When the legendaries bunch up, keep running: do not try to kill them after the third. From minute 35 Arceus breaks your item and speeds up; from minute 37 its touch hurts more and more. A realistic record sits between 5 and 8 minutes of Judgment.

With the Kecleon Shop it climbs to rank 931: Mud Shot via TM, Impish nature, Gluttony ability.

Level-up cards: priority

  1. Reflexes (−8% cooldown, up to 10): with low cooldowns the slows and stuns chain without gaps.
  2. Sprint: about 3 cards to reach the soft cap of 130 with Stride maxed.
  3. Armor (up to 12): the reduction stacks up to the 80% cap and saves the most HP against the legendaries.
  4. Vigor (+20 HP): margin against the legendaries’ hits, which scale 15% every 5 s.
  5. Synthesis (+0.9 HP/s): recover between pillar volleys (Arceus’ damage is percentage-based, only regeneration offsets it).
  6. Zidra Berry when below half: heals 45% instantly.
  7. Power at least once: it is the condition for Hyper Beam to evolve at max level.
  8. Reflexes at least once: it is the condition for Hyper Voice to evolve at max level.
  9. Power (up to 40) afterwards: damage barely matters in the Judgment. Magnet and Learning only if nothing else is left.

Move evolutions

  • Hyper Beam evolves at max level if you have taken the Power card at least once this run.
  • Hyper Voice evolves at max level if you have taken the Reflexes card at least once this run.
