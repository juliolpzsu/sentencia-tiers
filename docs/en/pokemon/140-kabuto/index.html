Verdict: Among the shortest-lasting in the Judgment. Play it for fun, not for the ranking. Backed by its 13 real runs plus the prediction. Among the worst at clearing hordes: reaching minute 30 is the real challenge.

Strategy

Judgment tier D and horde tier D: it reaching minute 30 is already a challenge. Weak to Grass (×2.25), Electric (×1.71) and Ground (×1.50). Resists Fire (×0.39), Normal (×0.59) and Poison (×0.67).

Before minute 30, farm: the level you arrive with is what extends the Judgment most. Fuse Rock Throw, Swords Dance, Water Gun and Rock Slide in slot 1 at every rift; keep Quick Attack in its own slot for when you get surrounded; Bubble and Rock Tomb in the remaining slots. Once the group is complete, skip further rifts.

Needs one Sprint card for the 130 cap. Small: pillars have less room to catch it.

Kecleon Shop: Ice Beam (TM), Impish Mint and Battle Armor ability (+7 s in the Judgment).
