Verdict: Marginal in the Judgment: it lacks control or toughness. Average at clearing hordes: reaches minute 30 with a normal run.

Strategy

Munkidori is Judgment tier C (rank 371 of 966) and horde tier B: it reaches minute 30 if the run goes cleanly. Against the legendary pool it is frail against the legendaries (it takes ×0.96 of the reference damage). What punishes it most: Ground (×1.67), Ghost (×1.59), Dark (×1.56). What it resists best: Fighting (×0.38), Poison (×0.58), Fairy (×0.61).

Its natural Judgment tools: Mist Ball slows the legendaries by 30% (they run at 80% of your speed: that is enough to keep them off you). Bosses ignore knockback and resist whirlpools, but slows land at 100% and stuns chain with Reflexes: with that you can keep the legendaries at bay while you dodge Arceus.

Speed: with Stride maxed it already touches the soft cap of 130; Arceus always runs at 1.22× your speed, so speed is for dodging and for keeping the legendaries (at 80% of your speed) off you, not for outrunning Arceus. It is small, so pillars (44 px radius) and beams have less margin to catch it.

Plan: stay 250–400 px from Arceus (its shockwave only fires if you are closer than 210), run wide circles along the arena edge and gain distance during its casting pauses (1.1–1.5 s every cast). When the legendaries bunch up, keep Mist Ball active on the group. From minute 35 Arceus breaks your item and speeds up; from minute 37 its touch hurts more and more. A realistic record sits between 5 and 8 minutes of Judgment.

With the Kecleon Shop it lands at rank 624 (it improves, but others improve more): Timid nature.

Level-up cards: priority

  1. Reflexes (−8% cooldown, up to 10): with low cooldowns the slows and stuns chain without gaps.
  2. Sprint: with Stride maxed it already sits at the soft cap of 130; one or two more still help you dodge.
  3. Armor (up to 12): the reduction stacks up to the 80% cap and saves the most HP against the legendaries.
  4. Vigor (+20 HP): margin against the legendaries’ hits, which scale 15% every 5 s.
  5. Synthesis (+0.9 HP/s): recover between pillar volleys (Arceus’ damage is percentage-based, only regeneration offsets it).
  6. Zidra Berry when below half: heals 45% instantly.
  7. Reflexes at least once: it is the condition for Mist Ball and Confusion to evolve at max level.
  8. Power at least once: it is the condition for Sludge to evolve at max level.
  9. Power (up to 40) afterwards: damage barely matters in the Judgment. Magnet and Learning only if nothing else is left.

Move evolutions

  • Mist Ball evolves at max level if you have taken the Reflexes card at least once this run.
  • Sludge evolves at max level if you have taken the Power card at least once this run.
  • Confusion evolves at max level if you have taken the Reflexes card at least once this run.
