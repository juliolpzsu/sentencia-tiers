Strategy

Bellsprout is Judgment tier B (rank 273 of 966) and horde tier C: it struggles to reach minute 30: it needs a good item and no risks. Against the legendary pool it is frail against the legendaries (it takes ×1.32 of the reference damage). What punishes it most: Ice (×2.00), Fire (×2.00), Psychic (×2.00). What it resists best: Grass (×0.45).

It has neither a natural ranged slow nor a stun: against the legendaries it can only run and endure, so the Judgment comes down to dodging and regeneration. The Kecleon Shop changes that (see the tab).

Speed: it needs about 2 Sprint cards to reach the soft cap of 130; Arceus always runs at 1.22× your speed, so speed is for dodging and for keeping the legendaries (at 80% of your speed) off you, not for outrunning Arceus. It is small, so pillars (44 px radius) and beams have less margin to catch it.

Plan: stay 250–400 px from Arceus (its shockwave only fires if you are closer than 210), run wide circles along the arena edge and gain distance during its casting pauses (1.1–1.5 s every cast). When the legendaries bunch up, keep running: do not try to kill them after the third. From minute 35 Arceus breaks your item and speeds up; from minute 37 its touch hurts more and more. A realistic record sits between 5 and 8 minutes of Judgment.

With the Kecleon Shop it climbs to rank 260: Jolly nature, Gluttony ability.

Held item

Leftovers RecommendedRecover 1.5% of your HP per second. Arceus’ damage is percentage-based and pure: percentage regeneration is the only thing that offsets it.
Focus SashA lethal hit leaves you at 1 HP (every 60 s). Saves you from a pillar or an Arceus touch until minute 35, when Arceus breaks your item.
Assault Vest−20% damage taken. Helps reach minute 30; in the Judgment it becomes redundant once Armor hits the 80% cap.

Only one per run: dropped by bosses and locked chests.

Level-up cards: priority

  1. Reflexes (−8% cooldown, up to 10): with low cooldowns the slows and stuns chain without gaps.
  2. Sprint: about 2 cards to reach the soft cap of 130 with Stride maxed.
  3. Armor (up to 12): the reduction stacks up to the 80% cap and saves the most HP against the legendaries.
  4. Vigor (+20 HP): margin against the legendaries’ hits, which scale 15% every 5 s.
  5. Synthesis (+0.9 HP/s): recover between pillar volleys (Arceus’ damage is percentage-based, only regeneration offsets it).
  6. Zidra Berry when below half: heals 45% instantly.
  7. Synthesis at least once: it is the condition for Energy Ball to evolve at max level.
  8. Power at least once: it is the condition for Sludge to evolve at max level.
  9. Sprint at least once: it is the condition for Poison Sting to evolve at max level.
  10. Power (up to 40) afterwards: damage barely matters in the Judgment. Magnet and Learning only if nothing else is left.
