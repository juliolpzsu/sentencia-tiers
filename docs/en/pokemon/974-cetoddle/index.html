Verdict: Lasts a while in the Judgment, but others do it better with less effort. Among the worst at clearing hordes: reaching minute 30 is the real challenge.

Strategy

Cetoddle is Judgment tier B (rank 146 of 966) and horde tier D: it reaching minute 30 is already a challenge; the Judgment is almost a bonus. Against the legendary pool it is frail against the legendaries (it takes ×1.31 of the reference damage). What punishes it most: Steel (×1.94), Rock (×1.91), Fighting (×1.91). What it resists best: Ice (×0.44).

Its natural Judgment tools: Ice Beam slows the legendaries by 70% (they are left almost standing); Freeze-Dry stuns them in zones. Bosses ignore knockback and resist whirlpools, but slows land at 100% and stuns chain with Reflexes: with those the legendaries stop being a threat and all that is left is dodging Arceus. If you earn a bond in a rift, fuse the slow and the stun into one slot so both stay active at once.

Speed: it needs about 2 Sprint cards to reach the soft cap of 130; Arceus always runs at 1.22× your speed, so speed is for dodging and for keeping the legendaries (at 80% of your speed) off you, not for outrunning Arceus. It is big: it counts as the maximum radius (18 px) and takes slightly longer to leave a pillar.

Plan: stay 250–400 px from Arceus (its shockwave only fires if you are closer than 210), run wide circles along the arena edge and gain distance during its casting pauses (1.1–1.5 s every cast). When the legendaries bunch up, drop Freeze-Dry on them and keep Ice Beam active on the group. From minute 35 Arceus breaks your item and speeds up; from minute 37 its touch hurts more and more. A realistic record sits between 5 and 8 minutes of Judgment.

With the Kecleon Shop it lands at rank 422 (it improves, but others improve more): Impish nature.

A sudden chill marked on the ground that freezes whoever it catches.
WhyTelegraphed zone that stuns. On bosses it only lasts 35% (0.73 s per cast), but with the reduced cooldown it chains: a stunned legendary neither attacks nor hurts you on contact.

WhyYour damage move to reach minute 30 and finish legendaries: it is physical, its strong side (1.0× of max), and the projectiles track the target and never miss. Damage matters little in the Judgment (each legendary’s HP scales with your own damage), so reliability comes first.

Deals no damage: builds (physical) ATTACK while active.

Held item

Leftovers RecommendedRecover 1.5% of your HP per second. Arceus’ damage is percentage-based and pure: percentage regeneration is the only thing that offsets it.
Focus SashA lethal hit leaves you at 1 HP (every 60 s). Saves you from a pillar or an Arceus touch until minute 35, when Arceus breaks your item.
Assault Vest−20% damage taken. Helps reach minute 30; in the Judgment it becomes redundant once Armor hits the 80% cap.

Only one per run: dropped by bosses and locked chests.

Level-up cards: priority

  1. Reflexes (−8% cooldown, up to 10): with low cooldowns the slows and stuns chain without gaps.
  2. Sprint: about 2 cards to reach the soft cap of 130 with Stride maxed.
  3. Armor (up to 12): the reduction stacks up to the 80% cap and saves the most HP against the legendaries.
  4. Vigor (+20 HP): margin against the legendaries’ hits, which scale 15% every 5 s.
  5. Synthesis (+0.9 HP/s): recover between pillar volleys (Arceus’ damage is percentage-based, only regeneration offsets it).
  6. Zidra Berry when below half: heals 45% instantly.
  7. Power at least once: it is the condition for Ice Beam and Freeze-Dry to evolve at max level.
  8. Reflexes at least once: it is the condition for Ice Shard to evolve at max level.
  9. Power (up to 40) afterwards: damage barely matters in the Judgment. Magnet and Learning only if nothing else is left.
