Verdict: Lasts very well in the Judgment. Backed by its 50 real runs plus the prediction. Clears hordes very well: reaches minute 30 without trouble.

Strategy

Judgment tier A and horde tier A: it reaches minute 30 without trouble. Weak to Fire (×2.21). Resists Poison (×0.28), Grass (×0.33) and Fairy (×0.55).

Before minute 30, farm: the level you arrive with is what extends the Judgment most. Fuse Meteor Mash, Swords Dance, Hyper Voice and Flash Cannon in slot 1 at every rift; keep Quick Attack in its own slot for when you get surrounded; String Shot in the remaining slots. Once the group is complete, skip further rifts.

Already at the 130 speed cap.

Kecleon Shop: Energy Ball (TM), Ice Beam (TM), Thunder Wave (TM) and Impish Mint (+43 s in the Judgment).
