Verdict: Among the shortest-lasting in the Judgment. Play it for fun, not for the ranking. A prediction: no real Judgment runs of it. Among the worst at clearing hordes: reaching minute 30 is the real challenge.

Strategy

Judgment tier D and horde tier D: it reaching minute 30 is already a challenge. Weak to Flying (×2.00), Fire (×2.00), Rock (×1.99).

Before minute 30, farm: the level you arrive with is what extends the Judgment most. Fuse Hyper Voice, Swords Dance, Hyper Beam, Signal Beam in slot 1 at every rift; Quick Attack apart for when you get surrounded; String Shot in the remaining slots. With the group complete, skip further rifts.

Needs 2 Sprint cards for the 130 cap.

Kecleon Shop: Adamant Mint → rank 747.
