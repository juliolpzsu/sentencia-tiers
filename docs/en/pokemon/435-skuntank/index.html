Verdict: Among the shortest-lasting in the Judgment. Play it for fun, not for the ranking. Mostly a prediction: few real runs. Weak at clearing hordes: reaching minute 30 is hard.

Strategy

Judgment tier D and horde tier C: it struggles to reach minute 30. Weak to Ground (×1.66), Flying (×1.07) and Water (×1.06). Resists Psychic (×0.51), Grass (×0.62) and Ghost (×0.64).

Before minute 30, farm: the level you arrive with is what extends the Judgment most. Fuse Sludge, Swords Dance, Poison Sting and Sucker Punch in slot 1 at every rift; keep Quick Attack in its own slot for when you get surrounded. Once the group is complete, skip further rifts.

Already at the 130 speed cap. Big: it takes longer to leave a pillar.

Kecleon Shop: Adamant Mint (barely changes how long it lasts).
