Strategy

Gholdengo is Judgment tier A (rank 40 of 966) and horde tier B: it reaches minute 30 if the run goes cleanly. Against the legendary pool it shrugs off the legendaries’ hits (it takes ×0.71 of the reference damage). What punishes it most: Dark (×1.49), Fire (×1.49), Ghost (×1.48). What it resists best: Normal (×0.28), Poison (×0.28), Bug (×0.33).

How to play it (like those who last longest): before minute 30 farming is what counts, because the level you arrive with explains the Judgment most. Slot 1: Shadow Ball, Nasty Plot, Hex, Shadow Sneak; fuse them at every rift (up to 4 in one slot, they fire together and the booster only works while active). On the cards, Power, Reflexes and Learning; Sprint only up to 130. Slot 2: Quick Attack. When enemies close in, switch to it (each dash leaves you invulnerable for 0.3 s; with 10 Reflexes and Quick Claw, nonstop) and switch back to your group once clear. With the group complete, skip further rifts: fusing frees slots and new moves come back on the cards instead of upgrades.

Speed: with Stride maxed it already touches the soft cap of 130; Arceus always runs at 1.22× your speed, so speed is for dodging and for keeping the legendaries (at 80% of your speed) off you, not for outrunning Arceus. It is small, so pillars (44 px radius) and beams have less margin to catch it.

In the Judgment what kills you is Arceus’ telegraphed attacks (pillars on you, cross beams and its shockwave if you are within 210 px) and its touch: all four ignore Quick Attack’s invulnerability. Keep moving, leave every circle as soon as it appears and gain distance during its pauses (it stands still 1–1.5 s every time it attacks); stay 250–400 px away and off the edge. When legendaries bunch up on you, Quick Attack. At minute 35 Arceus breaks your item. Those who last longest go past 30 minutes of Judgment, arriving at level 200 or more.

With the Kecleon Shop it lands at rank 40 (it improves, but others improve more): Thunder Wave via TM, Timid nature.

Abilities

Good as Gold Ability 1 · default+8% damage with Steel-type moves.

Each account rolls ability 1 or 2 at random (50%), and the hidden one 1 in 20. The Ability Capsule (1,500) swaps between 1 and 2; the Hidden Ability Capsule (5,000) sets the hidden one.

Held item

Quick Claw Recommended−20% cooldown: more damage to farm and, with Reflexes, Quick Attack chains its dashes and keeps you invulnerable nonstop against hordes and legendaries.
LeftoversRecover 1.5% of your HP per second. Arceus’ damage is percentage-based and pure: percentage regeneration is the only thing that offsets it.
Focus SashA lethal hit leaves you at 1 HP (every 60 s). Saves you from a pillar or an Arceus touch until minute 35, when Arceus breaks your item.

Only one per run: dropped by bosses and locked chests.

Level-up cards: priority

  1. Reflexes (−8% cooldown, up to 10): with low cooldowns the slows and stuns chain without gaps.
  2. Sprint: with Stride maxed it already sits at the soft cap of 130; one or two more still help you dodge.
  3. Armor (up to 12): the reduction stacks up to the 80% cap and saves the most HP against the legendaries.
  4. Vigor (+20 HP): margin against the legendaries’ hits, which scale 15% every 5 s.
  5. Synthesis (+0.9 HP/s): recover between pillar volleys (Arceus’ damage is percentage-based, only regeneration offsets it).
  6. Zidra Berry when below half: heals 45% instantly.
  7. Reflexes at least once: it is the condition for Shadow Ball and Shadow Sneak to evolve at max level.
  8. Magnet at least once: it is the condition for Hex to evolve at max level.
  9. Power (up to 40) afterwards: damage barely matters in the Judgment. Magnet and Learning only if nothing else is left.

Nature (Mint, 1,500)

Timid (+Speed −Attack): raises speed up to the soft cap of 130 and lowers the attack it does not use.

Ability (Capsule)
