Verdict: Lasts a while in the Judgment, but others do it better with less effort. Mostly a prediction: few real runs. Among the worst at clearing hordes: reaching minute 30 is the real challenge.

Strategy

Judgment tier B and horde tier D: it reaching minute 30 is already a challenge. Weak to Rock (×1.40), Bug (×1.40) and Ghost (×1.38). Resists Fighting (×0.32), Psychic (×0.52) and Grass (×0.53).

Before minute 30, farm: the level you arrive with is what extends the Judgment most. Fuse Confusion, Nasty Plot, Mist Ball and Hyper Voice in slot 1 at every rift; keep Quick Attack in its own slot for when you get surrounded; String Shot in the remaining slots. Once the group is complete, skip further rifts.

Already at the 130 speed cap. Big: it takes longer to leave a pillar.

Kecleon Shop: Modest Mint (+1 s in the Judgment).
