Verdict: Among the shortest-lasting in the Judgment. Play it for fun, not for the ranking. A prediction: no real Judgment runs of it. Weak at clearing hordes: reaching minute 30 is hard.

Strategy

Judgment tier D and horde tier C: it struggles to reach minute 30. Weak to Fighting (×1.94), Rock (×1.22) and Ground (×1.22). Resists Ghost (×0.35).

Before minute 30, farm: the level you arrive with is what extends the Judgment most. Fuse Hyper Voice, Swords Dance and Hyper Beam in slot 1 at every rift; keep Quick Attack in its own slot for when you get surrounded. Once the group is complete, skip further rifts.

Needs one Sprint card for the 130 cap.

Kecleon Shop: Ice Beam (TM), Rock Tomb (TM), Shadow Ball (TM) and Scrappy ability (+2 s in the Judgment).
