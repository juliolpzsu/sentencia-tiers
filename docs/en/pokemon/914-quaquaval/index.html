Verdict: Lasts very well in the Judgment: it has the control tools that matter. Average at clearing hordes: reaches minute 30 with a normal run.

Strategy

Quaquaval is Judgment tier A (rank 48 of 966) and horde tier B: it reaches minute 30 if the run goes cleanly. Against the legendary pool it is frail against the legendaries (it takes ×1.07 of the reference damage). What punishes it most: Flying (×1.60), Electric (×1.58), Psychic (×1.58). What it resists best: Bug (×0.59), Rock (×0.59), Steel (×0.59).

Its natural Judgment tools: Bubble slows the legendaries by 45% (they run at 80% of your speed: that is enough to keep them off you); Dynamic Punch stuns them in zones; Aqua Ring gives it regeneration against the pillars. Bosses ignore knockback and resist whirlpools, but slows land at 100% and stuns chain with Reflexes: with those the legendaries stop being a threat and all that is left is dodging Arceus. If you earn a bond in a rift, fuse the slow and the stun into one slot so both stay active at once.

Speed: with Stride maxed it already touches the soft cap of 130; Arceus always runs at 1.22× your speed, so speed is for dodging and for keeping the legendaries (at 80% of your speed) off you, not for outrunning Arceus. It is big: it counts as the maximum radius (18 px) and takes slightly longer to leave a pillar.

Plan: stay 250–400 px from Arceus (its shockwave only fires if you are closer than 210), run wide circles along the arena edge and gain distance during its casting pauses (1.1–1.5 s every cast). When the legendaries bunch up, drop Dynamic Punch on them and keep Bubble active on the group. From minute 35 Arceus breaks your item and speeds up; from minute 37 its touch hurts more and more. A realistic record sits between 5 and 8 minutes of Judgment.

With the Kecleon Shop it lands at rank 194 (it improves, but others improve more): Jolly nature, Moxie ability.

Held item

Focus Sash RecommendedA lethal hit leaves you at 1 HP (every 60 s). Saves you from a pillar or an Arceus touch until minute 35, when Arceus breaks your item.
LeftoversRecover 1.5% of your HP per second. Arceus’ damage is percentage-based and pure: percentage regeneration is the only thing that offsets it.
Assault Vest−20% damage taken. Helps reach minute 30; in the Judgment it becomes redundant once Armor hits the 80% cap.

Only one per run: dropped by bosses and locked chests.

Level-up cards: priority

  1. Reflexes (−8% cooldown, up to 10): with low cooldowns the slows and stuns chain without gaps.
  2. Sprint: with Stride maxed it already sits at the soft cap of 130; one or two more still help you dodge.
  3. Armor (up to 12): the reduction stacks up to the 80% cap and saves the most HP against the legendaries.
  4. Vigor (+20 HP): margin against the legendaries’ hits, which scale 15% every 5 s.
  5. Synthesis (+0.9 HP/s): recover between pillar volleys (Arceus’ damage is percentage-based, only regeneration offsets it).
  6. Zidra Berry when below half: heals 45% instantly.
  7. Sprint at least once: it is the condition for Bubble to evolve at max level.
  8. Power at least once: it is the condition for Dynamic Punch and Water Gun to evolve at max level.
  9. Power (up to 40) afterwards: damage barely matters in the Judgment. Magnet and Learning only if nothing else is left.
