Strategy

Dewott is Judgment tier B (rank 197 of 966) and horde tier C: it struggles to reach minute 30: it needs a good item and no risks. Against the legendary pool it is frail against the legendaries (it takes ×1.05 of the reference damage). What punishes it most: Electric (×1.73), Grass (×1.73), Psychic (×1.08). What it resists best: Steel (×0.65), Water (×0.65), Fire (×0.65).

Its natural Judgment tools: Bubble slows the legendaries by 45% (they run at 80% of your speed: that is enough to keep them off you); Aqua Ring gives it regeneration against the pillars. Bosses ignore knockback and resist whirlpools, but slows land at 100% and stuns chain with Reflexes: with that you can keep the legendaries at bay while you dodge Arceus.

Speed: it needs one Sprint card to reach the soft cap of 130; Arceus always runs at 1.22× your speed, so speed is for dodging and for keeping the legendaries (at 80% of your speed) off you, not for outrunning Arceus. It is small, so pillars (44 px radius) and beams have less margin to catch it.

Plan: stay 250–400 px from Arceus (its shockwave only fires if you are closer than 210), run wide circles along the arena edge and gain distance during its casting pauses (1.1–1.5 s every cast). When the legendaries bunch up, keep Bubble active on the group. From minute 35 Arceus breaks your item and speeds up; from minute 37 its touch hurts more and more. A realistic record sits between 5 and 8 minutes of Judgment.

With the Kecleon Shop it lands at rank 234 (it improves, but others improve more): Ice Beam via TM, Bold nature, Shell Armor ability.

Level-up cards: priority

  1. Reflexes (−8% cooldown, up to 10): with low cooldowns the slows and stuns chain without gaps.
  2. Sprint: one card to reach the soft cap of 130 with Stride maxed.
  3. Armor (up to 12): the reduction stacks up to the 80% cap and saves the most HP against the legendaries.
  4. Vigor (+20 HP): margin against the legendaries’ hits, which scale 15% every 5 s.
  5. Synthesis (+0.9 HP/s): recover between pillar volleys (Arceus’ damage is percentage-based, only regeneration offsets it).
  6. Zidra Berry when below half: heals 45% instantly.
  7. Sprint at least once: it is the condition for Bubble to evolve at max level.
  8. Power at least once: it is the condition for Water Gun to evolve at max level.
  9. Power (up to 40) afterwards: damage barely matters in the Judgment. Magnet and Learning only if nothing else is left.

Move evolutions

  • Bubble evolves at max level if you have taken the Sprint card at least once this run.
  • Water Gun evolves at max level if you have taken the Power card at least once this run.
