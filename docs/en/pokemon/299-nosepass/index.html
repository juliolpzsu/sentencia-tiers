Verdict: Marginal in the Judgment. A prediction: no real Judgment runs of it. Among the worst at clearing hordes: reaching minute 30 is the real challenge.

Strategy

Judgment tier C and horde tier D: it reaching minute 30 is already a challenge. Weak to Water (×1.44), Steel (×1.36), Ground (×1.30). Resists Normal (×0.50), Fire (×0.53), Poison (×0.54).

Before minute 30, farm: the level you arrive with is what extends the Judgment most. Fuse Rock Throw, Swords Dance, Rock Slide, Hyper Voice in slot 1 at every rift; Quick Attack apart for when you get surrounded; Rock Tomb in the remaining slots. With the group complete, skip further rifts.

Needs 3 Sprint cards for the 130 cap.

Kecleon Shop: Adamant Mint → rank 597.
