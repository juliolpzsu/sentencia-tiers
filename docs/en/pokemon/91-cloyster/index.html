Verdict: Lasts a while in the Judgment, but others do it better with less effort. Backed by its 50 real runs plus the prediction. Average at clearing hordes: reaches minute 30 with a normal run.

Strategy

Judgment tier B and horde tier B: it reaches minute 30 if the run goes cleanly. Weak to Electric (×1.45), Rock (×1.15) and Grass (×1.15). Resists Ice (×0.35) and Water (×0.59).

Before minute 30, farm: the level you arrive with is what extends the Judgment most. Fuse Water Gun, Swords Dance, Ice Shard and Hail in slot 1 at every rift; keep Quick Attack in its own slot for when you get surrounded; Ice Beam and Freeze-Dry in the remaining slots. Once the group is complete, skip further rifts.

Already at the 130 speed cap.

Kecleon Shop: Careful Mint (barely changes how long it lasts).
