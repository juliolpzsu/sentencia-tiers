Ranking method

Rank follows the estimated seconds a good player lasts in the Judgment. The estimate comes from two sources:

  1. Real records: the median of each Pokémon's best runs in the game's public tables (see the check below).
  2. Prediction for all: a ridge regression fitted on the Pokémon with records, from attack, size, horde rank, HP, speed, BST, legendary status and the six model components below. Out of sample it gets the order right with a correlation of 0.29; the old model score alone, 0.09.

They are blended on a log scale: the more real runs a Pokémon has, the more they weigh (half at 10 runs). 540 of the 966 have some. Tiers follow rank: S 1–30, A 31–120, B 121–320, C 321–620, D 621–966.

Records are biased: rare Pokémon are mostly played by veterans with more upgrades. The estimate is how long that Pokémon lasts in good hands, not how long you will last with any account.

The two variants

  • Default: ability 1, neutral nature and only its own types’ moves (plus the Normal pool everyone has). What any player has right after pulling it from the gacha.
  • Kecleon Shop: its abilities are tried (Capsule and Hidden Capsule), the natures that raise Defense, Special Defense or Speed while lowering the attack it does not use (Mint), and the TMs it learns in the games and the shop sells. The best-scoring combination is kept.

Assumptions

  • Permanent upgrades maxed for the Pokémon (Vitality +80, Stride ×1.24, Reflexes ×0.76, Critical Hit 30%) and typical cards by minute 30 (15 Vigor, 8 Sprint, 10 Reflexes).
  • Toughness = effective HP divided by the average damage taken from the pool of 86 legendaries: each hits with its primary type and its strong side (physical or special), applying the softened chart (×1.6 / ×0.6 / ×0.3), your matching defense and your ability.
  • Control = 55% the best slow (by percentage and coverage: a beam or an aura covers more than a melee hit) and 45% the best stun (duration ×0.35 on bosses against its cooldown).
  • Damage = best ranged same-side attack against the pool, with its ability. Deliberately light: the legendaries’ HP scales with your own damage.
  • Reaching minute 30 does not score: the ranking measures only how it lasts in the Judgment. How good or bad it is at clearing hordes is shown on each page (SurvivorTiers rank and tier, 441,000 simulated runs) and in the verdict.

Check against real records

On 2026-10-24 the game's public tables with the 50 best solo runs of each Pokémon were read, and for the 195 Pokémon with at least 10 runs past minute 30 the median seconds survived in the Judgment was computed. Runs above 20 minutes of Judgment, impossible under the current rules, were discarded. Only aggregates are kept, no player data.

What is not modelled

Items (recommended separately), the weather summoned by abilities, the player’s dodging skill and rift bonds. The weights are a judgment call, not a measurement: two Pokémon a few ranks apart are, in practice, equal.
